## Meeting notes in Google Docs

Shorthand can now write your meeting notes straight into Google Docs. Choose
Google Docs under Settings > Notes, sign in with Google, and pick one doc with
a tab per meeting or a folder with a doc per meeting.

- The notes fill in while the meeting runs, with a status line at the top that
  says when they were last updated and when they're done.
- Each meeting gets a short name chosen from what was said, with the date
  first, like "2026-11-25 - Q1 pricing review".
- Under the Note-taking prompts tab you can write your own instructions for
  the note taker and choose one for meetings and one for Assisted notes.
