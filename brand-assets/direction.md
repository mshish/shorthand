# Shorthand identity direction

Shorthand turns a fleeting voice into a written thing: the bird carries the thought, the fountain pen commits it, and the connected S makes that action the beginning of the product name. The clay treatment makes local software feel handmade and quietly helpful, while the small silhouette reduces the same story to the approved bird + pen contour when detail disappears.

- **Ink blue** — chosen for written confidence and trust, but shifted toward the bird's characterful ocean blue rather than generic software blue.
- **Coral** — chosen because it is the living, tactile counterpoint already present in the wing and underline; it marks the one thing happening now rather than signalling warning or success.
- **Warm paper** — chosen to make the interface feel written-on rather than displayed-on, without reproducing the raster's peach lighting so strongly that it harms reading comfort.
- **Blue-black / cream ink** — chosen to keep long reading calm and highly legible while avoiding clinical pure black and white.
